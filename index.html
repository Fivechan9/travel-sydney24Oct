06:10 AM

Land at Sydney Airport (SYD) & Head to Hotel

Easiest Transit: Take the Airport Train to Central Station, then transfer to the M1 Metro Line toward Tallawong and exit at Victoria Cross Station (right next to Vibe Hotel North Sydney). Or take a 25-min Uber/Taxi directly to 171 Pacific Highway.

08:30 AM

Drop Luggage & Relaxed Aussie Brunch

Leave your bags at Vibe Hotel reception. Walk 3 minutes to Goodfields Eatery (37A Miller St) or Sol Bread + Wine for a relaxed coffee and warm breakfast.

10:30 AM

Wendy Whiteley's Secret Garden (Lavender Bay)

A tranquil, shaded sanctuary just a 12-minute gentle downhill stroll from your hotel. Sit on a garden bench overlooking Lavender Bay and the Sydney Harbour Bridge without any crowds.

12:30 PM

Lunch at Kirribilli Village & Milsons Point Walk

Follow the flat harbourside boardwalk past Luna Park to Milsons Point (right underneath the Harbour Bridge with direct views of the Opera House). Have a casual lunch in leafy Kirribilli Village (try Celsius Coffee Co. on the wharf or fish & chips by the water).

02:30 PM

Hotel Check-in, Afternoon Nap & Easy Dinner

Take the train 1 stop from Milsons Point back to North Sydney. Check into your room, rest up after the overnight flight, and grab a casual dinner right downstairs at Glorietta (Italian at 100 Mount St) or Green Moustache.

Day 3 • Monday, October 26, 2026

Full Leisure Day: Iconic Harbour & Manly Ferry Cruise

No Webinar Today • 100% Free
Monday Scenic Roadmap (Effortless Ferry & Flat Walks)
Victoria Cross Metro The Rocks & Opera House F1 Ferry (Wharf 3) Manly Beach & Shelly Beach
09:30 AM

3-Minute Metro Under the Harbour

Sleep in at your own pace. Hop on the Metro at Victoria Cross and alight 1 stop later at Barangaroo Station (only 3 minutes!).

10:00 AM

Wander The Rocks & Circular Quay

Stroll through The Rocks historic cobblestone laneways and heritage buildings. Follow the completely flat waterfront promenade around Circular Quay right up to the steps of the Sydney Opera House and into the peaceful Royal Botanic Garden.

12:30 PM

Relaxed Waterfront Lunch

Dine outdoors at Opera Bar (right beside the Opera House looking at the Harbour Bridge) or grab casual gourmet bites at the Gateway Food Court at Circular Quay.

02:15 PM

Scenic F1 Ferry to Manly Beach

Board the iconic green-and-gold F1 Manly Ferry from Circular Quay Wharf 3. This 30-minute ride gives you the best harbour cruise in Sydney for the price of a regular bus ticket. In Manly, walk down The Corso to the ocean beach, grab gelato, and take the flat coastal path to calm Shelly Beach.

05:30 PM

Golden Hour Return Ferry & Dinner

Catch the ferry back to Circular Quay as the city lights come on. Enjoy dinner in the CBD or Darling Square before taking the Metro straight back to Victoria Cross.

Handy Sydney Travel Tips

  • No Transport Card Needed: Tap your contactless Visa, Mastercard, or Apple/Google Pay directly on Metro, train, bus, and ferry gates. Daily fares are automatically capped.
  • Groceries Right Next Door: Greenwood Plaza (connected to North Sydney Station, 3 mins walk from your hotel) has a large Coles supermarket, pharmacies, and casual lunch spots.
  • Spring Layering: Days are sunny and mild (20°C–23°C), but harbour breezes on the ferry and evenings dip to ~14°C. Keep a light jacket or sweater handy.
